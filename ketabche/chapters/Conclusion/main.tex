\chapter{Conclusion}
\label{chap:conclusion}

This thesis focused on the design and implementation of a personalized Retrieval-Augmented Fine-Tuning (RAFT) method for question answering and student response evaluation in educational environments. The primary objective was to develop and personalize a system tailored to each student, capable of delivering customized answers based on their individual knowledge background. This final chapter summarizes the work, revisits the research questions with answers grounded in our findings, and reflects on the journey undertaken throughout this research.

\section{Summary of Contributions}

This thesis makes the following key contributions:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{A dataset of student questions.} This dataset comprises questions collected from students via forum requests. Each question was subsequently answered with accurate and verified knowledge to ensure the completeness and reliability of the dataset.

    \item \textbf{A dataset of question-answer pairs with knowledge background annotations.} This dataset pairs each question with its corresponding answer and the student's knowledge background, making it suitable for the fine-tuning stage. It contains over 150 entries.

    \item \textbf{A complete RAFT and RAG pipeline.} As a result of this work, both a  RAG pipeline and a RAFT pipeline have been developed. These pipelines can be deployed on full-scale systems, such as the ALEA platform, for testing with students. The RAG pipeline can also be applied to more advanced models to evaluate how results vary with model capacity.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Revisiting the Research Questions}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{RQ1} \ref{RQ1}: How can the system dynamically adapt its responses to align with a student's current knowledge level and learning progress? The RAFT method demonstrates that the system can identify the student's level of understanding and tailor the answer based on their knowledge base. However, this does not provide a completely solid foundation for the model to fully address the student's needs.
    \item \textbf{RQ2} \ref{RQ2}: How can course-specific content and pedagogical materials be effectively integrated to ensure domain-relevant and accurate responses? After fine-tuning, the model exhibits a significantly better understanding of course-specific content, leading to more consistent and domain-relevant answers.
    \item \textbf{RQ3} \ref{RQ3}: What techniques and architectural strategies can be employed to effectively reduce hallucinations in large language model-based tutoring systems? The fine-tuning and RAFT pipeline demonstrate that the model achieves a better contextual understanding, which effectively mitigates hallucinations.
    \item \textbf{RQ4} \ref{RQ4}: How can the system generate more accurate, context-aware, and educationally valuable answers that support meaningful learning? The answer is visibly affirmative. Compared to the baseline, the model produces significantly more coherent and contextually grounded responses. Furthermore, within the RAFT and RAG pipelines, the model demonstrates the ability to identify prerequisite concepts and explain them within the context of the given topic.
\end{itemize}

\section{Limitations}

A significant limitation encountered during the writing of this thesis was the sudden and unforeseen restriction of access to high-performance computing (HPC) resources in Sudan, which occurred without prior notice. As a result, all work and experimental results stored on the HPC system were lost. This incident took place approximately three weeks prior to the submission date, and recreating the results using alternative tools that lacked the necessary processing power, GPU access, and model execution capabilities proved exceedingly challenging on a short time. This was an extremely difficult and regrettable experience that substantially impacted the progress of this research. Only the datasets was safe from this catastrophic happen because they storages under my own PC and I didn't use HPC and to create them however the augmentation of questions that I want try in the process was lost.
