
\section{Embedding Models}
\label{sec:embedding-models}

Embedding models play a critical role in RAG and RAFT pipelines, as they directly influence the quality of retrieved documents and, consequently, the overall system performance. In this thesis, multiple embedding models are evaluated to determine which achieves the best retrieval quality, and different embedding dimensions are investigated for their effect on the retrieval score in the experiments.

One key observation from the preliminary experiments is that embedding models with dimensions exceeding 1000 tend to produce degraded retrieval performance and retrieve a larger number of irrelevant or semantically distant documents. These findings suggest that higher-dimensional embeddings do not necessarily lead to better retrieval and may even introduce noise into the similarity search process.
