\section{Introduction}

The introduction of Large Langue Models (LLMs), such as ChatGPT, has changed learning methods by providing students with an accessible platform to understanding topics. However, the "one-size-fits-all" nature of these models introduce a big risk in real-world education environment because students profile and background knowledge is very different and personal. Also, LLMs lacks the required adoption to align with specific course content and topics, and does not cover all course details in their memory. on other hand education is a very high level risk, a grade can change the hole life of a person, the possibility for LLMs hallucinated on their results is unacceptable. Therefore, filling these gaps is essential for the responsible implementation of LLMs in academic era.
